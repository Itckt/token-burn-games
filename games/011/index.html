Radar offers rain rate and Doppler wind. Teal wind moves toward the marked antenna and amber wind moves away. Choose a site or use the open probe as the antenna. The probe shows a dash at the antenna and the eye. Radar rings are 30 km apart.

Compare puts clouds on the left and the selected map on the right. Drag the handle or focus it and use the arrow keys. Sky lighting sets the sun or moon angle in Cloud view, including during a comparison.

With the probe open, tap a point within 120 km of the eye or use a placement button. Shift + ↑ moves north. Dragging the scene still orbits.

The life cycle curve follows four stages. Scrub to freeze an instant. Change the warning signal to take manual control from that point.

Wind field arrows point with the surface flow. Colour shows speed in km/h. Section cuts away the near side of the clouds and rain. Rotate its axis and drag across the profile to sample the amber line.

In the field notebook, capture A and B to compare readings. Inspect a slot to pause at its saved time and probe position. Capturing again replaces that slot.

In Pressure, lower pressure sits deeper. Pale contours are 10 hPa apart. Orbit the bowl or tap its surface with the probe.

In the probe tray, choose a travel time and trace a parcel. The coral trail uses the wind at release. Probe arrival samples its destination in the current storm.

The station recorder samples a fixed location during playback. Stop to save its CSV. Seeking a different storm instant finishes the log.
