Survey tools

Choose Plot route and mark two points on the map. You can also move the crosshair with the arrow keys, hold Shift for fine steps, and press Enter to mark each end. Esc cancels the plot.

Drag across the elevation profile or focus it and use the arrow keys to inspect a station. Tour starts there and follows the terrain. The blue area shows water above the lakebed.

Probe adds a centre crosshair and draws contours every 2 m, with a heavier line every 10 m. Aim at ground to read elevation, slope and the downhill compass direction. Aim at open water to sound its depth. The diamond on the map locates the reading.

Lake navigation

Turn on Depth chart to see soundings in colour on the lake and map. Ochre marks water shallower than the skiff’s 1.5 m draft. Choose Skiff to launch on nearby water. Use W/S for power and reverse, and A/D to steer. On touchscreens, use the arrows. The helm shows speed and depth.

Choose Chart passage and mark two points in navigable water. The planner finds a channel around shoals. Choose Skiff to cast off at A facing the passage. Follow the striped buoys to the jade finish buoy. Press F to take flight.

Gate relay

Choose Start relay below the profile to line up with the first gate. Use the Fly controls to pass through each gold ring. Your first movement starts the clock. Read your gate splits in the viewer and race your best time on the current course. Open the guide to pause, or choose End relay to leave the course.

Basin study

Compare the original Fresh slopes with Talus and Worn. Each moves loose rock downhill. Adjust Lake level to flood the banks or expose the bed. The map shows the shoreline and the readouts measure all ground below that level. Choose 0 m datum to return to the original shore.

Choose Trace runoff and pick high ground on the map, or use the arrows and Enter. A blue line starts at the nearest mesh vertex and follows the slope to water, a hollow, or the survey edge. Moving chevrons show the downhill direction in 3D. Read the drop in the elevation profile or choose Fly route to follow it.

Choose Sea mist for low visibility or Squall for wind-driven rain. Weather keeps changing when the clock is held.

Ridge observatory

Open Ridge observatory beneath the clock to survey the skyline from your camera, the station selected on the route profile, or the highest point you can fly to. Drag the bearing marker or use its arrow keys, then choose Face this bearing to fly from that station. The sun strip shows when the sun’s centre clears the mapped terrain. Scrub it or jump to the first or last sunlit minute. Face the sun holds that minute and points the camera along the sunlight.

A preset and seed always recreate the same landscape. Try Kowloon-Tong for an after-class flight.
