    Play an algorithm
    Enter face letters in the editor. Use ′ for reverse, 2 for a half turn, and (R U)3 for a repeated group.
    Bracket notation
    [A,B] plays A, B and their inverses in that order. [A:B] plays A, B, then the inverse of A. Either part can contain several turns.
    Follow one piece
    Choose its home letters under the face net or Alt-click a 3D corner or edge between turns. Alt-click it again to clear. The net rings its stickers and the 3D view lights its body.
    Inspect the layers
    Toggle Explode to separate the pieces. Click an outward face to turn it or Alt-click an exposed edge or corner to track it.
    Practice a cross
    Choose a target face in Cross coach. An aligned edge matches both centers. Show hint finds a shortest route. Play the suggested turn or try it yourself.
    Retry a position
    Select a checkpoint slot and save at any completed turn, including a step in playback review. Restore brings back its cube and move history.
    Race a mix
    Enter a code such as 3-HKBU in Time trial. Your first manual turn starts the clock. Solving stops it. Trial turns animate at a fixed speed.
