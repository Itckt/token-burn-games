    How to explore

    Pick a café object or its illustrated index entry to open an order slip. Turn on the pins to find the smallest pieces.

    Take a seat puts you at eye level. Drag or use the arrows to look around. Stand up or Space returns you to the diorama. In a close-up, the arrows beside Wide view visit another object.

    Pick the Shop cat and call it to a resting spot. Its pin and close-up follow it. Pick the Booth radio to tune three original pieces and choose Listen for sound.

    Pick the Window booth to raise or lower all three striped blinds. On the French toast slip, choose the cut and finish the guest asked for, then send the plate.

    Pick the Counter till to open the drawer. Count the guest’s change from the printed bill. Each coin button shows how many are left. Use Undo coin to put the last coin back, then hand over the change.

    Take a ticket to work the counter. Follow each clue by choosing a model, pin or index entry. A correct choice stamps the ticket. You can put it down and resume later.

    Tea bench brings the front cup into view. Pour four measures to match the recipe, then serve. Use Undo to take back the last pour.

    Use Fan to change the draft through the steam. Postcard frames your view and the selected slip for a PNG you can save.

    Keyboard shortcuts work while the scene is focused.
