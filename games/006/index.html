    Key guide

    Select a target speed to depart. Assisted stops brake and board automatically. In manual mode, use the S board and brake cue. The train levels the final 8 m with its doors locked.

    ↑ W
    Raise target by 10 km/h
    ↓ S
    Lower target by 10 km/h
    Space
    Brake to a standstill
    C
    Coast with traction off. Press again to resume the selected target. Stop protection stays active.
    D
    Open or close doors at a station
    M
    Toggle manual stops while berthed. Follow the cab cue and press Space to stop within 1 m of the S board.
    G
    Switch signal working on or off at a station. Amber warns of a red signal ahead. The train brakes and waits for green, then resumes your target.
    Z
    Enable line speed limits at a berth. Read the boards on the right. Supervision brakes for the amber speed and releases at the green 80 board.
    J B
    Enable guard working at a berth with J. Open with D, let people cross, then close with D. When the lamp turns white, B rings two bells and clears departure.
    N
    Cycle the service clock through evening, last train and first train. Ten seconds between stations advances the clock by one minute.
    P
    Pause or resume the ride
    T
    Toggle rain. The wiper runs automatically on a wet viaduct.
    X
    Sweep the windscreen once, including inside a tunnel.
    ← →
    Look left or right. Drag for a free look.
    V
    Switch between the cab and platform views
    L
    Look through the left window for the counter-running campus shuttle on either viaduct.
    I
    Take a seat in the saloon. The overhead display names the stop and reports signal holds and door movements. Drag to look around from your seat.
    R
    Recenter the cab view
