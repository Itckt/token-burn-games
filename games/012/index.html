One day per second advances the simulation by one Earth day every real second. Distances in AU use the mean Earth–Sun separation as one unit.

In Orbit study, choose an apsis to pause there. The two colored sectors each cover one twelfth of a revolution in time. In Signal range, choose a destination and turn on Map link to see the connection.

Follow Earth to see the Moon orbit. Select the Moon to open its eyepiece. In the observatory, choose a station and target, then move the time slider or select a rise, transit or set. Choose Use time in atlas to pause the main clock at that instant.

In Signal range, Frame pair keeps both endpoints in view. Motion trails mark six equal time intervals into the past. Open the encounter planner to scrub their separation, select a turning point, or save a field note. Visit in atlas applies the previewed pair and pauses at its exact time.
