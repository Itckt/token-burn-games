    Click or tap a piece in the 3D view, use the course profile, or choose a piece under Build at. Cards insert before it. Earlier and Later move it along the run. Click the tabletop or finish bay to append again.

    In Time trials, Start heat ranks three releases by elapsed time. Follow marble tracks the newest runner. Use Playback in Marble setup to slow a loop down, or pause and step through it.

    Try the Gap jump (9) on Podium Post at 7 and 10 m/s. Open Run replay in Time trials to see the landing and speed trace. Drag the replay slider to pause at any instant. P plays, the period key steps a paused replay, and Escape returns to the held race.

    Try the starter loop at 10 m/s. Drive strips (7) add speed and brake strips (8) slow a fast marble. A ramp placed at the lowest track height climbs uphill.

    In Build, set Gate height between 0.9 and 12 m and choose Apply. The ramps rebuild from that height. Undo restores the previous tower.

    Cross the blue halfway gate and the flag going forward to post a time. Open Time trials to see the latest drop race the ghost. Clear or edit the course to reset its records.

    Use Layouts → Track blueprints to copy a course or open a saved code.
